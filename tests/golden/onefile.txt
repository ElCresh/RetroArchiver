
dskdiff 1.2 - compares several reads of the same floppy

  usage: dskdiff FIRST.DSK SECOND.DSK [THIRD.DSK ...] [-c CYLINDERS]

  With two reads it shows the differences byte by byte.
  With three or more it tells how many variants each track has
  and whether the reads have a majority.

  The disk format is detected automatically (40, 80, other).
  Use -c to force it if the detection is wrong.

exit=2
