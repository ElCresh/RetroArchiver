  In 1 of them the SIZE DIFFERS from one read to the next.
  In an EDSK a track grows when SAMdisk saves several copies of a
  sector that gave different data on later rereads. It means that
  SAMdisk ran into errors there and had to insist, even if it did
  not tell you on screen: it is the sign of degraded media.
  Check with:  samdisk scan FILENAME.DSK
  and look for the m1, m2 or dc flags on the cylinders listed above.

  If the reads report dc or nd errors, the media is degraded:
  reread with --retries=30 --rescans=5, clean the heads, try another drive.

  If instead they report NO errors, these are weak sectors written
  to be unstable on purpose, that is, copy protection. In that case
  no amount of retries helps: you need a flux capture.

  Where some reads agree you can keep the majority variant.
