RetroArchiver - Third-party notices
===================================

RetroArchiver archives retro software collections (3.5" floppy disks,
CDs and DVDs) on a PC with Windows XP SP3. Its setup program,
RetroArchiver-Setup.exe, includes and installs the third-party work
listed below. Items 1 to 4 and 6 are redistributed exactly as their
authors publish them, without modifications: the setup only unpacks,
copies or runs the original files, whose SHA-256 checksums are listed in
installer/third-party.txt in the source repository. Item 5, BearSSL, is a
source library compiled into RetroArchiver.exe, so it has no line in that
list: its sources are in third_party/bearssl in the source repository,
unmodified.

The full license texts are installed in the "licenses" folder next to
RetroArchiver.exe, and each program's license is also installed next to
that program in the "tools" folder.
