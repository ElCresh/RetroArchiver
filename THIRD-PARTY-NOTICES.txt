RetroArchiver - Third-party notices
===================================

RetroArchiver archives retro software collections (3.5" floppy disks,
CDs and DVDs) on a PC with Windows XP SP3. Its setup program,
RetroArchiver-Setup.exe, includes and installs the third-party programs
listed below. They are redistributed exactly as their authors publish
them, without modifications: the setup only unpacks or runs the original
files, whose SHA-256 checksums are listed in installer/third-party.txt in the
source repository.

The full license texts are installed in the "licenses" folder next to
RetroArchiver.exe, and each program's license is also installed next to
that program in the "tools" folder.
